# Quality Rubric

Score or gate each shot on these dimensions before assembly.

## Semantic accountability

PASS only if a viewer can identify the visible evidence for the narration. For abstract concepts, the visible relation/change must correspond to the claim, not merely share its mood.

## Information gain

Each shot/beat must add something: new state, relation, comparison, scale, viewpoint, decision or consequence. Repeated atmospheric footage with no new information is a failure.

## Concept discrimination

When narration contrasts high/low frequency, same/different source, known/unknown, AI/human role, etc., those categories must be visually distinguishable without labels.

## Continuity

Check recurring identity, palette, world rules, spatial topology, screen direction, motif meaning and start/end states. Continuity may intentionally reset at a motivated cut; accidental drift is not a style choice.

## Executability

Prompt/shot must describe filmable actions and visible results. Abstract verbs without physical manifestation fail.

## Composition

The intended subject/relation should dominate the frame. Decorative effects must not compete with the explanatory object.

## Motion

Camera and subject motion should reveal information or carry causality. Unmotivated drifting, zooming or orbiting is noise.

## Prompt density

One generation unit should have a small number of ordered events. If the model must simultaneously invent a scene, maintain identity, perform several actions, change camera grammar and land on a precise final state, split or simplify.

## Technical gates

Probe actual output for resolution, fps, frame count, duration and audio. Track SHA and provenance. Do not upscale or subtitle a BLOCK take.

## Decision

- BLOCK: wrong meaning, contradictory concept, wrong provenance, severe continuity or unusable artifact.
- RETAKE: semantic core exists but execution/composition/motion/identity is insufficient.
- TRIM: good source take, only timing/cut issue.
- PASS: production-ready for assembly/postprocess.
